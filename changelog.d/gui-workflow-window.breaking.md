The GUI is redesigned as one workflow window in place of the six-step wizard: Home (start from a measured trace, a simulated scan or a synthetic pulse; open a session; recent sessions), then Data (load the trace and check its marginal), Prepare, Retrieve and Analyse (Overview, Traces, Pulse, Diagnostics, Dispersion and Uncertainty pages), with one primary action in the top bar and the session's settings or a replay script on request. Plots are interactive pyqtgraph views with hover readouts, zoom and figure export through matplotlib; forms fold by section and say what the focused setting does. `croak.gui.run_wizard` is now `croak.gui.run_gui` and `croak.gui.Wizard` is now `croak.gui.MainWindow`; the `croak-gui` command is unchanged.
