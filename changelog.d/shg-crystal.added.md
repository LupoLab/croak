SHG in a Type I crystal: `croak.crystals.CrystalSetup` (cut angle with an external tilt or the wavelength the crystal was tuned to, the crossing geometry from `croak.geometry.TwoBeamGeometry`, and the fundamental's polarisation relative to the crossing plane), passed as `material` to the forward models for SHG, propagates the signal near 2ω0 as the extraordinary wave and so models phase matching, group-velocity mismatch and the ω/n generation prefactor. `croak.crystals.depth_points` gives the depth nodes the phase mismatch needs, With the beams crossing in the crystal's principal plane the signal's tilt makes the trace asymmetric in delay; the model includes it, to first order, as opposite delay drifts of the two arms (`delayed_beam` sets its sign, which flipping mirrors the trace in delay), and `check_tilt` warns where the remainder matters (#24).
