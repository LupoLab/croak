The marginal checks and the SHG marginal correction can predict the marginal through a Type I crystal: `croak.crystals.shg_marginal` gives the crystal model's delay-summed marginal, and `predicted_marginal`, `predicted_centroid` and `apply_marginal_correction` take a `croak.crystals.PhaseMatching` (crystal and thickness) with the pulse carrier. `load_and_clean(marginal_correct=True, marginal_crystal=...)` corrects a measured trace onto the crystal's marginal, removing the instrument's spectral response while keeping the phase matching for the crystal model, and records it as `TraceData.marginal_crystal`. A retrieval now refuses a corrected trace only when its medium differs from the one the correction used, instead of refusing every corrected trace with a crystal. Sessions correct through the retrieval's crystal, and the GUI shows the crystal and beam settings on the Data stage, where the Check step uses them. See [Marginal consistency checks](docs/howto/marginal_checks.md#through-an-shg-crystal) (#25).
