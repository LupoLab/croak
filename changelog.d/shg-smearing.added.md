Two-beam delay smearing for SHG FROG: `croak.smearing.shg_two_beam_kernel` builds the delay blur of two Gaussian beams from a `croak.geometry.TwoBeamGeometry` with a `beam_diameter` (rms width w0 tan(alpha) / (sqrt 2 c) = lambda d / (sqrt 2 pi D c), after Taft et al. 1996), and the forward models, solvers and smearing fit accept it for SHG, with or without a crystal; `croak.smearing.check_kernel` refuses a kernel with a shape parameter for SHG. Sessions take `beam_diameter_mm` and build the kernel from the crystal's own beams at the pulse's wavelength, and the GUI's Beam geometry group has the diameter, the smearing switch and a width read-out. See [Two-beam SHG](docs/howto/geometric_smearing.md#two-beam-shg) (#24).
