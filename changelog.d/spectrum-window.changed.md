The independent fundamental spectrum is windowed by its own wavelength range, `lams_lims` in `load_and_clean` (λs in the Prepare stage; `lams_min_nm`/`lams_max_nm` in a session), instead of the trace window λm mapped through the interaction's carrier scaling. It defaults to the spectrum's full extent, so an SHG spectrum is no longer cut by twice the trace window, and the spectrum's outer wings now reach the spectral regularisation target and the initial guess unless λs is narrowed. This applies to PG and SD too: a saved session that narrowed λm, and so also tapered its spectrum, reads back with λs unset (the spectrum's full extent); set λs to taper the spectrum again (#23).
