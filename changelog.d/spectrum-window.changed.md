The independent fundamental spectrum is windowed by its own wavelength range, `lams_lims` in `load_and_clean` (λs in the Prepare stage; `lams_min_nm`/`lams_max_nm` in a session), instead of the trace window λm mapped through the interaction's carrier scaling. It defaults to the spectrum's full extent, so an SHG spectrum is no longer cut by twice the trace window, and the spectrum's outer wings now reach the spectral regularisation target and the initial guess unless λs is narrowed (#23).
