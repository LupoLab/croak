The independent fundamental spectrum is windowed by its own wavelength range, `lams_lims` in `load_and_clean` (λs in the Prepare stage; `lams_min_nm`/`lams_max_nm` in a session), instead of the trace window λm mapped through the interaction's carrier scaling. It defaults to the spectrum's full extent, so an SHG spectrum is no longer cut by twice the trace window, and the spectrum's outer wings now reach the spectral regularisation target and the initial guess unless λs is narrowed. This applies to PG and SD too: `load_and_clean` without `lams_lims` no longer tapers the spectrum at λm. Session files are now `options_version = 3`; a session saved by an earlier croak reads back with λs set to its λm mapped to the fundamental (×2 for SHG), so it prepares its spectrum exactly as before (#23).
