`auto_third_order_exponent` takes `trace_window` (the trace marginal's wavelengths) and `spectrum_window` (the independent spectrum's; default its full range, where `window` used to apply 210-1000 nm to both) in place of the single `window`, and the marginal-check stage has a trust region for each. The spectrum's region in the GUI defaults to the spectrum's full range, measured or not, so a spectrum crossing 1 µm is no longer clipped at 1000 nm; the trace's region still defaults to 190-1000 nm for a measured trace. Replace `window=w` with `trace_window=w`, and pass `spectrum_window=w` as well to reproduce the old behaviour exactly (#23).
