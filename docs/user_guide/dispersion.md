# Propagation and dispersion tuning

A retrieved field refers to a particular location in the optical system.
Known material paths and mirror responses can be used to propagate it to
another plane or explore compression. Distinguish this operation from including
the nonlinear medium in the retrieval's [forward model](models.md).
